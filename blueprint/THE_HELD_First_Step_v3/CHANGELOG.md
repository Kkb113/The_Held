# v3.0 / The First Step — baseline changes

This is a replacement production baseline for the approved revised concept, not a small patch to v1.

- Nine-area, 225-minute route replaces eight-area progression. Forty sequence cards and 31 puzzle concepts use new stable IDs.
- Living bearers supply enforced balance/correction; powered machinery supplies force. Hold does not stop time or store unlimited energy.
- Swimming, bounded buoyancy, breath states, a movable air bell and a retrieval dock that tips and empties its air pocket are included.
- Paired balance controls are rehearsed in a carrier and a domestic room, then used by the occupied Walking Ward. The final act is embodied traversal rather than stationary node selection.
- An authored support spine and receiving pads bound Ward mechanics. Incidental interior physics supplies reactions without deciding route solvability.
- The twelve-person survivor cohort is explicitly tracked and independently evacuated. No infinite unseen-city evacuation or commanded-human army is implied.
- Anja's irreversible root integration differs from removable peripheral harnesses and Ivo's open service cradle.
- The final root governor is unloaded before Ivo takes it. Deliberate release is protected against pause, focus loss, disconnection, remapping and quit/reload.
- Nineteen durable facts and atomic checkpoint recovery prevent duplication, replayed irreversible moments or Anja's resurrection after escape failure.
- Water and Ward risks are gated before bulk content. The quality slice includes the full Laundry plus a separate First Step benchmark.
- Registers now specify 164 asset families, 40 systems, 211 animation/pose tasks, 52 audio families, 21 milestones, 131 unexecuted tests, 18 risks and 20 first-sprint tasks.
- Four JSON Schemas, three examples and a planning-data validator support engineering handoff. They are not a playable Unity project.
