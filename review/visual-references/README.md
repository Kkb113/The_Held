# The Held — visual references and design principles

Recorded 7 September 2026. Companion to the existing blueprint and production review.

The user's two prototypes are the primary visual guidance for The Held. INSIDE images are secondary references for execution quality. This document preserves the blueprint's story, world, characters and Hold rules. Visual interpretations below are design recommendations, not additional instructions inferred from image contents. The original blueprint is unchanged.

## Primary guidance: the user's prototypes

### H01 — Laundry bridge

![The Held prototype: hand fitting and suspended bridge](<C:/THE HELD/review/visual-references/prototypes/THE_HELD_01_Laundry_Bridge.png>)

Preserve the composition principle: Ivo and the fitting on the left; the suspended mechanism occupying the central negative space; the destination visible on the right. Hanging cloth, wheeled laundry carts and maintenance hardware give this industrial space a human purpose. A small warm contact area holds attention within cool gray depth. Dark foreground objects frame the room without filling the playable lane.

Production interpretation: show the fitting, its physical connection, the bridge's pivot/support and the receiving edge clearly enough to explain cause and effect. The image alone cannot establish how the bridge moves or which process Hold restrains. Use the existing room design to define that behavior before reproducing the composition. A still must not accidentally imply that Hold lifts or repairs a bridge. Keep falling droplets and loose cloth active when they are outside the held process.

### H02 — Harbor cover

![The Held prototype: Ivo sheltered in a harbor lane](<C:/THE HELD/review/visual-references/prototypes/THE_HELD_02_Harbor_Cover.png>)

Preserve the low, vulnerable viewpoint, layered retaining walls, receding utility poles, distant crane, limited warm windows and isolated searching light. Ivo's crouched pose and the concrete barrier communicate a practical survival action. The distant figures create anticipation while coastal infrastructure establishes place.

Production interpretation: distinguish the playable lateral lane from scenery receding into depth. A convincing perspective street must not promise free movement into the background. Make actual cover agree with enemy visibility tests; darkness and scenic occlusion are not substitutes for that agreement. The high hand fitting reads well compositionally, but its exact height and access require validation against the existing acquisition rules. Distant figures remain references for scale and tension, not new enemy designs or behaviors.

## Working visual principles for The Held

These principles implement the user's prototype guidance alongside blueprint §08. They should guide subsequent concept art, blockouts, assets, lighting and camera reviews.

| Area | Direction | Practical review criterion |
|---|---|---|
| Composition | Dark foreground, readable gameplay plane, softer background story; simple large masses | Ivo, immediate route, mechanism and consequence remain identifiable at gameplay scale |
| Camera | Controlled side-on perspective with authored depth and look-ahead | A composed frame remains usable while running, crouching, jumping and reversing direction |
| Scale | Small child within maintained domestic and civic infrastructure | Controls and maintenance access obey believable human dimensions despite monumental backgrounds |
| Palette | Cool, restrained atmosphere; scarce warm practical accents | Preserve charcoal, damp green-gray, porcelain, oxidized brass and weak amber from the blueprint |
| Materials | Painterly masses with selective tactile detail | Concentrate cracks, wear, roughness and repair marks around contact and narrative surfaces |
| Wetness | Localized reflections and water history | Reflections support form; they never erase a platform boundary or turn every room into polished black water |
| Atmosphere | Mist separates depths; practical light reveals selected space | Near-field gameplay stays clear across exposure, fog and display checks |
| Interaction | Physical ceramic hand fittings, visible connections, mechanical state | A fitting stays recognizable without bloom or color alone; Hold state reads through stopped process, vane and sound |
| Character | Vulnerability through weight, proportion and responsive contact | Read hand reach, brace, crouch and foot placement at normal camera distance |
| Threat | Human silhouettes and purposeful movement within infrastructure | Cover, patrol direction and exposure agree with gameplay rules; visual ambiguity does not conceal essential rules |
| Environmental story | Laundry, repairs, inhabited rooms, civic evacuation hardware | Every room reveals its original use and how restraint has altered ordinary life |
| Motion | Local arrested process surrounded by ordinary motion | Rain, cloth, breath and eyes continue when not mechanically part of the stopped process |

### How to interpret prototype details

- **Warm hand motif:** retain its visual prominence and tactile hand-shaped design. Treat the intense glow as a prototype emphasis, with a restrained practical treatment to be validated in-engine. It does not establish a magical glowing player hand or replace the porcelain material identity.
- **Character clothing:** the pictured muted reddish top is not a confirmed redesign. Keep the blueprint's Ivo identity and ash-blue jacket as the working baseline; carry over the prototype's small scale, simple silhouette and expressive poses.
- **Wet industrial mood:** appropriate to these scenes, but not a universal chapter filter. Preserve green-gray Laundry, domestic wallpaper and warm slices, civic glazing, hydraulic scale, and the pale daylight ending.
- **Bridge and cover:** preserve the framing lessons. Exact geometry, timing, mechanical ownership, fitting reach and patrol logic must come from the corresponding room specification and playable validation.
- **Image polish:** use the prototypes as lighting and composition targets. Their reflections, softness and small debris do not define an engine feature list or justify hiding important edges with blur.

### First implementation checks

1. Build a matching-camera graybox of an existing Laundry room. Show Ivo, fitting, suspended process and destination in one understandable composition.
2. Apply the prototype's broad value groups and restrained material palette. Compare blockout and lit screenshots at the same camera position.
3. Run the actual Hold, transfer, support and release states. Verify that each state changes the relevant body, mechanism, indicator and sound coherently.
4. Exercise a blueprint cover sequence using H02's depth and atmosphere. Test crouched and standing visibility, full patrol cycles, approach and retreat, plus no-audio readability.
5. Review motion and stills at normal play size and 1280×720. Inspect grayscale, darker ordinary displays, full mechanism travel, foreground occlusion and alternate aspect ratios. A strong still is insufficient if gameplay breaks its composition.

No new story, enemy, puzzle or mechanic is approved by this reference board. Its purpose is to make the existing game visually concrete while retaining the user's prototypes as persistent guidance.
