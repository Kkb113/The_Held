Updated 8 September 2026. [The v3 master](../../blueprint/THE_HELD_First_Step_v3/THE_HELD_First_Step_Master_v3.md), especially its visual-direction section, now governs this board. ## Preserved user prototypes

![Laundry bridge prototype](<C:/THE HELD/review/visual-references/prototypes/THE_HELD_01_Laundry_Bridge.png>)

Keep the readable relationship between Ivo, the fitting, bridge, support and destination; restrained warm attention within cooler depth; cloth and maintenance equipment that explain human use. V3 supplies the bridge's actual owner, lever pin and relief behavior. It must remain understandable throughout movement, not only in this still.

![Harbor cover prototype](<C:/THE HELD/review/visual-references/prototypes/THE_HELD_02_Harbor_Cover.png>)

Keep the small vulnerable figure, strong cover silhouette, distant institutional threat and layered civic infrastructure. Make the lateral playable route unambiguous and make cover geometry agree with detection. Background depth must not promise an inaccessible route.

Both images predate the new story. Follow v3's ash-blue Ivo and broad matte surfaces. Translate the luminous hand emphasis into readable porcelain, mechanical vane, cable tension and pose; do not add a glowing fist or beam. Use wetness locally where justified, without universal mirror floors, noisy photoreal concrete or blur that conceals puzzle edges.

## Review in a playable build

At the root roadmap's assigned phase, compare identical blockout and lit camera views, then run the room. Verify silhouettes, hand/foot contact, range and support indicators, backdrop depth, occlusion, alternate aspect ratios and ordinary darker displays. Test wrong actions as well as the solution. Allow environmental mystery while keeping immediate causes and consequences readable.

FS01 establishes domestic identity in the actual kitchen. FS04 is the first integrated Laundry visual benchmark; FS14–FS15 review the larger Laundry and occupied-Ward results before bulk expansion. This board does not establish a separate development queue.
