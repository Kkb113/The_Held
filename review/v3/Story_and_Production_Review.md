## The complete causal story

The old surge damaged the town. Motors, counterweights and hydraulics provide mechanical force, while living attendants supply enforced balancing corrections. The Relief Office perpetuates the emergency by replacing exhausted people instead of completing repairs and evacuation. The technology responds to load; institutional choices cause the captivity. The calm outside sea does not make retained water and unstable structures harmless.

Ivo starts beside Anja in their apartment. Moving a chair and helping latch a cover establish ordinary temporary help. Two taps, her cuff and the cup/spoon establish recurring recognition. The alarm follows player progression. On the landing Anja Holds the failing structure for him; Custodians secure the transport connection before taking her below. He follows with the dropped brace, believing she may be relieved elsewhere.

The Upper Terrace teaches direct Hold and release through the shutter, rotating tread and pursuit door. The Gardens broaden the language: autonomous wind and wildlife, water diversion and a load-sensing Porter whose rescue procedure becomes dangerous. The Laundry makes external transfer convenient, then gives that convenience a human source. Bridge pins, frame latches and drained mass remove loads; the fingernail supplies the first undeniable bodily clue.

At the Exchange, public life sits above constrained bodies. Their compensation follows real loads while eyes and attention remain independent. A jack relieves six people; the prepared cart permits a one-Hold escape from an exposed-harness Custodian; a stair pawl frees Orren. He travels independently, with the missing button and bent shoe supporting later recognition.

The Counterweight Cut exposes scale and reverses the relationship: a small carrier follows Ivo's balance. A properly received fall makes a route, and resumed motion defeats another Porter situation. The Drowned Street makes Ivo physically vulnerable again. Swimming, a guided air bell and an obsolete tipping dock teach that safety can be moved, but cannot be assumed. Tipping spills air; simple submergence is not the bell's failure rule.

The Burden House reveals the backs of familiar mechanisms. Previously relieved laundry attendants are unloaded but still confined. A lived-in room follows Ivo's shoulders, rehearsing the Ward without revealing it. Anja is irreversibly incorporated into the root. Her cuff and taps establish identity, and she refuses Ivo's replacement. Physical support can ease her strain and free other intact people; it cannot restore her body.

The Unfinished Sea reveals both the ordinary low sea and the retained danger. Ivo aligns a deflector, clears blockage and tests a limited discharge. He prepares receiving pads and the return culvert. The familiar hostel must move because the fixed crossing has failed. Its open maintenance cradle is visibly different from Anja's incorporated collar.

Actual movement intent begins the First Step. The camera reveals the hostel's two chimneys and pale curtain as occupied architecture follows the controls already learned on carrier and room. Ivo negotiates a narrow support, lowers the building beneath a girder, then kneels onto permanent abutments. Twelve people leave independently. Mechanical locks take the weight and let him leave the cradle. His power is temporary responsibility, with no combat or destruction fantasy.

Ivo returns through the prepared short culvert to the same Anja/root entity. Discharge proof, supported Ward and completed evacuation make the remaining governor manageable. After handover, a fresh deliberate input arms final release. Two taps and an open hand precede the player's chosen moment. Release ends Anja's suffering and her life; water follows the prepared route. Escape failure cannot resurrect her or replay the goodbye. Orren holds an ordinary gate, freely. Ivo accepts another survivor's hand and walks the final meters with unsynchronized steps.

The promise is this visible cohort's survival, not the rescue of an unseen entire city. Six Exchange people + three Laundry attendants + two Burden House bearers + Orren = twelve unique slots.

## Assessment

The revised design has a strong relationship between story and action. Arrest, assignment, physical relief, reciprocal balance, movable shelter, occupied relocation and voluntary help each change the meaning of the same small input vocabulary. Its strongest improvement is that the climactic responsibility is embodied in traversal. The air bell and domestic room prepare that change mechanically and emotionally.

The intended psychological horror depends on trustworthy physical rules. Conscious eyes, an unchanged breath and previously heard rhythms provide the disturbing evidence. Random malfunction, a surprise power removal or an unclear drowning boundary would weaken the argument. The ending needs players to understand both Anja's irreversible condition and the preparation that makes release safe.

The plan is concrete enough for controlled implementation, but it is not fully executable as written. The following issues need bounded contract decisions and build evidence. A planning validator cannot resolve them. The Ward's quality/cost, water fairness and animation capacity are substantial risks. A 120-week staffing scenario is not a delivery estimate for this workspace.
