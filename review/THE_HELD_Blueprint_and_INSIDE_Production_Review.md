# The Held blueprint and production review

7 September 2026. Review of Production Package v1.0. Contains the complete ending.

The Held has a coherent identity and a promising relationship between action and story. Preserve its existing concept. The next step toward the requested quality is to prove that its physical relationships are readable, its interactions remain convincing under interruption, and its emotional discoveries survive actual play. More machinery, more monsters, or more graphical technology would not establish those qualities.

This review separates **blueprint facts**, **documented reference practices**, **analysis**, and **proposed execution work**. Recommendations here do not amend the canonical blueprint, authorize production milestones as complete, or certify an unbuilt game. The original package was left unchanged.

The accompanying [sequence review](<C:/THE HELD/review/THE_HELD_Sequence_Review.md>) covers all 48 sequences individually. The [master blueprint](<C:/THE HELD/THE_HELD_Master_Blueprint_v1.md>) remains the design authority.

## 2. The Held's story and creative identity

The town has normalized an emergency that should have ended. Years earlier, a harbor surge led to people being attached to structural and hydraulic restraints. Their temporary effort became permanent infrastructure as institutions substituted replacement bearers for actual repair and evacuation. The technology's origin can remain mysterious. Its operation and its consequences must be understandable.

Ivo is thirteen, resourceful, and familiar with repairs. Anja, his mother, maintains external fittings and believes attendants are relieved in rotation. She does not understand central assimilation. During a fresh local collapse, she arrests their landing so he can cross. Custodians transfer her load to a transport rig and take her below as a replacement for a failing central bearer. Ivo follows with the dropped portable brace.

His early successes teach him to arrest motion and leave it with anchors. The comfort of having free hands conceals another person's effort. The laundry's inhale, exposed fingernail, domestic wall cavities, and Orren's release turn that suggestion into evidence. In the Passenger Hall, a restrained Custodian's moving eyes establish continued awareness. The Burden Rooms connect familiar mechanisms to particular people.

Anja has already been physically integrated into the central installation. Her cuff and two taps identify one person across separated structural contacts. Ivo can relieve a local load but cannot restore her body. She refuses his replacement. At the seawall, he sees the suspended surge and the much lower ordinary sea together: the external storm is over, but the town has kept its dangerous consequences suspended.

Ivo prepares discharge, stabilizes a deflector, and establishes an evacuation bypass. He temporarily becomes the central junction, using familiar station controls to let an established survivor cohort cross. Resolving physical dependencies contracts the network and enables his withdrawal before permanent integration. Anja retains the root until the final handover. Ivo takes its last local restraint, deliberately releases it, and starts the prepared discharge. Anja dies; the loss is neither reversed nor reclassified as a dream.

The escape returns to ordinary movement. Orren, previously freed through a mechanical support, holds an ordinary gate voluntarily. Ivo joins the survivors, uncurls his hand, accepts another hand, and takes playable final steps. Unsynchronized footsteps complete the movement from coerced collective strain to individual human lives.

The dramatic structure is especially strong because the player's operative question evolves:

| Campaign stage | Practical question | Emotional change |
|---|---|---|
| Evacuation and Laundry | How can I stop this and get through? | Urgency, competence, relief, unease |
| Houses and Passenger Hall | Who carries it, and what happens when I let go? | Recognition, complicity, dread |
| Burden Rooms and Flood | What can actually be repaired, and what cannot? | Grief, revised purpose, threatened identity |
| Last Bearer | How can I remove these dependencies so people can leave? | Constrained power and responsibility |
| Until Relieved | Can I release what my work has made safe to release? | Loss, relief, voluntary connection |

Protect the differences among the characters. Ivo is a child who understands tools, not an action hero. Anja is a loving worker with limited institutional knowledge, not a secret architect of the system. Orren is a recognizable beneficiary who later chooses to help, not a permanent escort. Custodians are constrained humans carrying out a disturbing procedure, not interchangeable supernatural pursuers. The Porter is a rescue worker trapped in an apparatus, with hoses and machinery creating his silhouette. Bearers are people. The root is Anja-linked architecture, with no separate monster identity or boss logic. These distinctions come directly from master §§3.3–3.5 and 7.

The Held's identifying materials and gestures already support this story: white ceramic hand fittings, repaired domestic surfaces, oxidized brass, cloth, breathing architecture, the doubled cuff, two taps, and the curled/open hand. They should govern concept approval and production decisions throughout the game.

The Hold contract is part of that identity, not a replaceable visual effect. The following distinctions are established in master §4; numeric values remain provisional.

| Rule | Meaning that production must communicate |
|---|---|
| Arrest a connected process | Stop its coordinate progression. Awareness, unrelated rain/dust and permitted secondary motion continue. Hold cannot rewind, heal or repair. |
| One personal process | Ivo can move, crouch, jump and make a short one-handed catch while holding. A heavy push or two-handed ladder requires free hands or external ownership. Rejection cannot silently drop his load. |
| Acquisition and retention differ | Acquire an exposed compatible contact within 3.2 m, facing and visible. Retain within 6 m despite ordinary occlusion; vane cues begin at 5 m, click at 5.7 m, with a 0.4-second break warning. Conduit reach requires a real authored path. |
| Release resumes the process | Resume captured phase/allowed velocity and subsequent motion. Do not accumulate motor work, replay elapsed simulation, or make longer Holds cause arbitrary force explosions. |
| Transfer changes the owner | Validate and commit atomically. An anchor's free socket still has a bearer; it accepts one compatible load. Releasing the personal input afterward does not release that anchor. |
| Support removes the need for restraint | Contact, orientation and a lock/catch or other physical resolution carry the load. Supported, Disconnected and Resolved describe different facts and must persist coherently. |
| Stations retain a defined assembly | Latches preserve branch state during selection. Station capacity is supplied by the interface, not unlimited personal power. Teach the context before Chapter 7. |
| Strain is narrative presentation | Authored stages change posture and breath without hidden speed penalties or permanent corruption from experimentation. Integration requires physical installation. |

The moment-to-moment loop therefore runs from reading a route and obstruction, through testing a process, to observing its owner or support, traversing, and encountering a consequence. Later puzzles deepen the relationships within that loop. They do not need a separate combat, crafting, swimming or inventory system.

## 3. Why the reference systems work together

The central production lesson is consistency of information. A shape suggests an action; movement explains a force; sound makes that force present beyond the visible frame; the player's contact confirms it; a consequence changes what the player believes about the world. If those signals disagree, the player starts investigating the software instead of inhabiting the situation. This is my synthesis of the evidence below, not a quoted Playdead design doctrine.

### Composition, lighting, color and detail

**Documented practice.** Marek Bogdan describes INSIDE's lighting as individually authored layers for effects including bounce, specular, rim, occlusion, and shadow. The constrained camera made precise control possible. His before/after cargo-hall example visibly changes the hierarchy of the same geometry: the dark enclosing frame recedes, localized lower-room detail becomes selective, and bright distant openings emphasize small human silhouettes. This is evidence for deliberate emphasis, not simply increasing darkness. [The Lighting of INSIDE](https://blog.playdead.com/articles/the_lighting_of_inside/lighting_of_inside.html)

**Why it matters.** Attention is limited. If the route, irrelevant pipework, background story, and active mechanism all have equal contrast, the player spends effort deciding what matters. A controlled image can make a physical problem readable while allowing its wider meaning to remain unresolved. Relative value and placement matter more than a blanket desaturation filter.

**Application.** The Held already specifies distinct depth layers and a porcelain/brass/domestic palette (§8). Each playable composition should contain a readable route and the parts needed to understand its current problem. Keep the support contact, moving endpoint, and relevant bearer response within one view or a clearly connected pair of views. Put atmospheric complexity behind that relationship. Light the full motion envelope, including invalid but recoverable configurations. A port that disappears halfway through a rotor cycle is an interaction defect.

**Documented detail.** Bogdan's separate detail article shows coordinated lamp flicker, apparent translucency, moving highlights, grounding shadows on moving objects, and small visual responses linked to other events. Sparse scenes make such details conspicuous. [The Weight of Detail](https://blog.playdead.com/articles/the_weight_of_detail/weight_of_detail.html)

**Application.** For The Held, spend this attention on porcelain flex, a pawl seating, fabric tension, a foot losing and regaining purchase, and a human exhale when support arrives. Ordinary rain and dust continuing around an arrested process are essential rule communication. Additional unrelated movement should not drown that contrast. Produce representative looks for domestic front/back, Laundry, civic hall, burden contact, flood vista, and final daylight before expanding kits. These are approval studies for the existing direction, not new biomes.

### Character movement, animation and interaction

**Documented practice.** Playdead's linked animation slides, in Danish, describe the tension between direct control and fluid motion; short clips, blends and interruptions; code-driven locomotion; prediction of contacts; breathing and gaze layers; limb placement; and responses to rejected actions. They also describe numerous context variants. These are translated summaries of accessible slide text, not a viewed demonstration or measured controller. [Animation and computer games, slides 9–15 and 19–30](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1QN99FZUQl3KE4c7eg9-GLLEl3Og0FqreJbiTrCc8ZAk/htmlpresent)

**Why it matters.** A vulnerable body needs visible intention and effort. A responsive control system lets the player identify with that intention. Weight becomes convincing through contact, posture, momentum and recovery; arbitrary delay merely creates uncertainty about whether input registered. Interrupted motion is the normal case in play, so transition quality is as important as the quality of individual clips.

**Application.** Preserve controller authority and Ivo's four narrative strain stages. Add a production coverage matrix: start, sustain, reverse, cancel, fail, resume, save/restore, and hand availability for every interaction family. Test reaching while moving, stopping beside a handle, trying a ladder while holding, turning on a carrier, and leaving a supported mechanism. Give rejected actions a brief bodily explanation without forcing a long repeat animation. Keep right-brace/left-gesture asymmetry through retargeting and mirroring.

The 162-clip allocation is a useful initial budget, but cannot predict the necessary transition coverage. INSIDE's animation counts are not comparable units and should not become a replacement quota. Reserve animator/engineer time for contact correction and variants that playtests actually expose.

### Physics and environmental interaction

**Documented practice.** The Huddle session abstract describes an integrated combination of physics bodies, animated motion, dynamic arms, and specialized shading. It establishes that a convincing creature can emerge from coordinated systems. It does not establish the implementation of every INSIDE puzzle or the boy's controller. [Huddle up session](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1024126/Huddle-up-Making-the-SPOILER)

**Why it matters.** Players build causal expectations: this mass rotates around that hinge; a trolley rolls downhill; a support takes a load. Believability requires reproducible relationships and convincing contact. Unrestricted simulation is not intrinsically more believable if it jitters, breaks a solution, or cannot reload consistently.

**Application.** Retain The Held's three physics tiers (§10.5): authoritative bounded processes, contact presentation, and incidental simulation. A machine's coordinate, phase, velocity, ownership and support condition determine gameplay. Cloth, hoses and loose fragments enrich the result. They cannot become accidental mandatory puzzle tools. Never freeze the whole object hierarchy when the fiction requires eyes, breath, rain, or secondary fabric to continue.

Author a release policy for each process family: motor phase resumes, gravity resumes, physical stop catches, or a supported endpoint remains still. The important test is whether the same readable setup produces the same intended outcome after short/long Holds, interruption, reload, and frame-rate variation. Do not claim cross-machine bitwise physics determinism.

### Puzzle design, readability and level progression

**Evidence boundary.** Playdead's published LIMBO design session explicitly concerns challenge, accessibility, frustration and learning through failure. It is relevant studio background, not proof that INSIDE used an identical puzzle methodology. Precise INSIDE room algorithms and checkpoint intervals were not established by this research. [Balancing Fun and Frustration](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1013665/Limbo-Balancing-Fun-and-Frustration)

**Analysis.** Minimal explanation works when the environment makes a useful hypothesis possible, an experiment produces interpretable feedback, and the player retains that learning for the next arrangement. Removing tutorials without doing this work produces obscurity. Good difficulty can come from predicting a relationship between familiar elements, without adding another input or hiding the answer in decorative noise.

**Application.** The blueprint's six reasoning families are appropriate: timing/orientation, allocation, connection, support, behavior after release, and dependencies (§6.1). Review adjacent sequences by what the player reasons about, not which machinery they depict. P09, P10 and P15 should respectively emphasize changed balance, an explicit human release, and support of an assembly. P16–P17 should read as one connected dependency problem with an intermediate success, not thirteen minutes of nearly identical cranking.

Each puzzle needs a visible objective, known starting motion, discoverable valid contact, comprehensible constraint, recoverable experiment, observable success, and a durable checkpoint at the reasoning milestone. Keep broad tolerances and accept valid configurations. Author reach envelopes and sightlines before final art. A 3.2 m acquisition range and 6 m retained connection are proposed design values, not proof that any room geometry works.

### Camera, transitions and cinematic presentation

**Evidence and analysis.** The first-party lighting material supports deliberate work within known camera conditions. The animation material supports prioritizing player response. Neither provides an exact INSIDE camera-follow algorithm. The transferable principle is to control the timing and spatial availability of information while preserving intelligible player action.

**Application.** The Held's camera should settle when the player is comparing causes, anticipate the next decision during pursuit, and widen in safety when a larger relationship becomes meaningful. C06-S01 must show both water levels together; C05-S04 must preserve recognition while expanding the apparent extent of Anja; C07 must retain orientation between Ivo, branches and survivor refuges. A smooth but disorienting network camera fails its purpose.

At the provisional 4.2 m/s run speed, the blueprint's 1.2-second warning view corresponds to about 5.04 m of travel. This is a derived blockout check, not a sufficient reaction-time guarantee: the player must also perceive and understand the choice. Check both travel directions, 16:9/16:10, lowest supported resolution, and the chosen ultrawide policy.

Build each chapter seam with a spatial connection, persistent state handoff, camera blend, acoustic transition and scheduled content load. Keep control while it is safe. When a short aligned gesture takes control, save/skip must reach the same semantic outcome as natural completion. Avoid treating a slow corridor as permission to let the loader stall.

### Enemies, chases and psychological horror

**Evidence boundary.** This review has not established INSIDE's internal AI state machines or pursuit parameters. Treat the following as encounter analysis and The Held recommendations. In broader first-hand horror guidance, Thomas Grip describes anticipation, narrative context, player agency and a credible world as central, while noting how mechanical exploits can break fear. His recommendation for ambiguity does not require making The Held's puzzle contracts ambiguous. [9 Years, 9 Lessons on Horror](https://frictionalgames.com/2019-10-9-years-9-lessons-on-horror/)

**Why it matters.** A threat becomes meaningful when the player can anticipate its presence, recognize its intention and understand the consequences of contact. If repeated failure exposes arbitrary detection or scripted teleportation, attention shifts from danger to manipulation of the game system. Uncertainty about motives can coexist with dependable local rules.

**Application.** Keep the Custodian's covered/exposed equipment distinction physical. Telegraph perception through head, torso and approach; telegraph capture with readable reach and lane contact. Preserve awareness through a Hold. The P13 trolley must delay a released worker through a visible obstruction, allowing the player to predict future behavior. Maintain one CoverDefinition for wet-sheet appearance and AI sensing. The mechanical light shutter changes a demonstrated exposure condition; darkness alone must not unpredictably confer immunity.

Keep the Porter a limited rescue cycle. Its reach/secure/retract behavior explains the threat and its tragedy together. Preserve its basket as a stable carrier during Hold. Do not turn it into a repeated boss or add new immunities to sustain difficulty.

The Held's most distinctive horror is recognition: a fitting contains a person, the person remains aware, a public service depends on coercion, Anja cannot be restored, and Ivo's competence makes him suitable replacement material. Allow observation after each important inference. Do not follow every ambiguity with a sting or an attack. The player's grief should grow from understanding; early mandatory tutorials should not be framed as a hidden morality failure.

### Sound, ambient sound, music, silence and audio tension

**Documented practice.** Andersen describes composing each area's sound and music together, allowing recognizable sources to carry additional expressive meaning. In the interview he discusses industrial textures, controlled perceived intensity, and layered movement sounds. This supports a unified soundscape rather than a final music track laid over completed effects. [Expressive Machines interview](https://blog.prosoundeffects.com/expressive-machines-martin-stig-andersens-award-winning-game-audio-for-inside)

**Documented implementation.** The audio slides show varying inhale/exhale events, animation feedback, gradual alignment with running steps, emotion/action/intensity parameters, and special handling of scene changes. They also describe profiling entire playthroughs and inspecting recorded output for glitches. These historical implementation examples are not settings to copy directly into a new backend. [Unbreaking Immersion, slides 10–22, 26–45 and 47–58](https://schmid.dk/talks/2016-06-16-wwise/schmid-Wwise_2016-INSIDE_Audio.pdf)

**Why it matters.** Sound carries events outside the frame and makes distance, material, effort and impending movement perceptible. If a machine's sound becomes a person's strain after transfer, players hear continuity of cause. If tension simply adds louder layers, long puzzle attempts become exhausting and relief has nowhere to go.

**Application.** Use the blueprint's motion-to-body relationships as a small authored vocabulary. Every key family needs running, arrested, personal, anchor/station, supported and released treatment. Preserve process rhythm or another recognizable feature through source changes. Distinguish Ivo's bodily sound from the distant bearer's, then remove the relevant tension on relief. Anja's separated proxies share identity and breathing phase. Independent survivor footsteps after the root release are the payoff to the prior common rhythm.

Before a Custodian enters, portal-filtered equipment and footsteps can establish approach. Their category and position must agree with AI events and visual responses. Do not give essential-only audio warnings. Sound captions must convey useful evidence without naming the hidden bearer ahead of the discovery. A low-volume mix must preserve important clicks and motion cues without flattening all depth.

Compose pauses by subtraction: retain air, fabric, small contact, and breath while removing redundant machinery and score. Test five-minute dwell and repeated respawn so loops do not become obvious. Keep critical danger timing in the game simulation, as the blueprint requires. Let audio shape noncritical presentation through validated requests; do not let device latency decide whether a gate kills Ivo.

### Pacing, checkpoints, technical presentation and polish

**Documented practice.** Playdead's performance slides describe spatially divided gameplay/environment content, controlled visibility and initialization, savepoint profiling, input playback, and automated performance inspection. The slides explicitly acknowledge that recorded play can drift because the game is not fully deterministic. They frame technical invisibility as an experiential aim. [Stutter-free 60 FPS, slides 5–20 and 35–65](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1dew0TynVmtQf8OMLEz_YtRxK32a_0SAZU9-vgyMRPlA/htmlpresent)

**Why it matters.** A hitch at a reveal draws attention to asset loading. A broken contact draws attention to a rig. A long retry turns dread into rehearsal. These are emotional failures as well as technical ones. Average frame rate and a polished opening are insufficient evidence of campaign quality.

**Application.** Retain short encounter recovery and durable reasoning milestones, with a separate checkpoint after the final release. Carry ambient continuity across restart, while resetting hazardous processes and local warning cues to the restored phase. Profile the fastest route, reverse traversal, repeat death at a seam, the flood, and the network camera. Keep an active Hold's endpoint resident or transfer responsibility explicitly to a valid proxy.

Use the existing CPU/GPU p95 allocations, p99/spike tracking, memory budgets and low-setting checks as provisional gates, not measured results. Add a motion-quality reel at the same checkpoints: hands, feet, thin ceramic lines, cloth, moving shadows, fog gradients and abrupt Hold/release. Temporal ghosting on a fingernail can destroy narrative readability even when a frame-time graph is excellent.

## 5. A concrete example of the quality bar

P10, **The staircase and Orren**, is the best early test of the whole game's causal and emotional promise (§5 C03-S04; §6.2). Keep its authored steps and forgiving catch.

At entry, the camera establishes the suspended stair, service contact, ratchet and the person whose posture corresponds to its angle. Give the useful relationship priority over room decoration. The first observation should answer what blocks progress without requiring the player to understand the institution yet.

On takeover, the stair stays arrested while effort shifts from Orren toward Ivo. A contact sound and local vane state confirm the transfer; the animation does not become the owner transaction. Orren remains attached and safe. Releasing to the service catch teaches that a temporary interruption does not remove the load.

At a valid angle, Ivo operates the one-handed lever. Show the broad pawl entering the tooth and the resulting structural contact. When he releases, the weight settles onto the ratchet, the vane slackens, and Orren uncurls. A specific sustained strain layer disappears. Orren clears the footplate before traversal and the persistent rescue state commits with his safe position.

This sequence passes the intended quality bar only when an unfamiliar player can connect the support to the man's freedom, the contact looks right in motion, the sound reinforces the same event, an early mistake is recoverable, and the exact result survives reload. It can fail through lighting, timing, collision, animation, audio, narrative staging, or persistence even if every asset is individually attractive. That is why discipline integration is the relevant comparison with INSIDE.

The single combined character-artist/animator role faces significant contact and narrative work, while audio and QA support are external to the eight-person core. Measure person-days for mechanisms, interactions, rooms, rework and integration at the slice; keep reusable-tool cost separate from repeat room cost. Do not equate more generated assets with less integration work. Nothing inspected justifies a cost, launch date or INSIDE-equivalent quality guarantee.
