# The Held review of all 48 sequences

7 September 2026. Companion to [the production review](<C:/THE HELD/review/THE_HELD_Blueprint_and_INSIDE_Production_Review.md>). Contains full story spoilers.

Every sequence below was read in the [master blueprint](<C:/THE HELD/THE_HELD_Master_Blueprint_v1.md>) and its full register representation was checked against that source. The observations are execution priorities within the existing design. They do not replace the room cards or claim that any room has passed a playable test. Times are the blueprint's first-play allocations.

## Chapter 1 The Evacuation

Measure whether players discover the action and recognize the mother-child connection without making them wait for the minute marks. |

| C01-S03 — The useful shutter — 4 min | First local arrest and resumed motion. | Establish the contact and motion with the tray demonstration. Test release at multiple phases, short/long Hold, retained range through the crossing and an interrupted acquisition. Distinguish a readable machine cycle from a four-minute wait for the intended experiment. |
| C01-S04 — The tilted drum — 4 min | Orientation and moving-platform confidence. | Keep valid angles visibly broad; check footing, platform departure velocity and the exit view. ## Checks that apply across every room

Keep an entry snapshot, valid solution trace, alternate/recoverable state list, exit snapshot and intended player inference beside each room card. Record both what must be seen and what must remain ambiguous. Run the same room with sound muted, low graphics, assistance settings, keyboard/controller, reverse movement and checkpoint restoration. Separate failure to understand a relationship from failure to execute an understood action.

For animation and sound, test dwell and interruption as well as a smooth intended playthrough. For art, test the darkest point and all moving endpoints. For threat, test perception and capture from valid occlusion and lane states. For continuity, replay callbacks using saved support, ownership and survivor identity rather than a fixed cinematic default.

The common acceptance question is whether the player can read a physical relationship, act through it, and recognize a consequence whose meaning develops across the campaign. The exact blueprint should remain the reference while these results are tested.
